Rose Niousha

PhD Student · UC Berkeley EECS

I am a PhD student in the Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences department at UC Berkeley, advised by Prof. Narges Norouzi.
My research broadly focuses on improving intelligent tutoring with AI. Recently, much of my work has focused on human simulation with LLMs in educational settings, particularly how to build student models that not only mimic student actions but also reason like students.

Prior to my PhD, I completed my Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science and Communications Engineering at Waseda University in Tokyo, Japan, where I also grew up.

Outside of academia, I enjoy cooking and weightlifting :)
